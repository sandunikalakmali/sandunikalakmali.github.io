Two collaborative papers presented through the **2025 IEEE 19th International
Conference on Industrial and Information Systems (ICIIS 2025)** are available
in the 2026 IEEE proceedings. An exact online-publication day is not stated
here because it has not been independently verified.

- [Feature Conditioned Diffusion for Audio Generation](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICIIS69028.2026.11450741)
- [Multispectral Imaging and Machine Learning for Soil Classification through Moisture-Induced Spectral Dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICIIS69028.2026.11450634)

The audio paper compares Mel, MFCC, CQT, KLT and combined conditioning
representations across speech, environmental-sound and music datasets. The
soil paper addresses a separate question from moisture regression: it studies
how moisture-induced spectral dynamics can support classification of soil
types.
