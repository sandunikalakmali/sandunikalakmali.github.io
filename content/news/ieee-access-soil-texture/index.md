Our peer-reviewed article, **“Reflectance Multispectral Imaging for Soil
Grain-Size Composition Estimation and USDA Texture Classification,”** was
published in *IEEE Access* on 6 August 2026 (volume 14, pages 128744–128763).

The work uses a custom 13-band reflectance multispectral system to estimate
clay, silt and sand composition and to classify controlled soil mixtures using
the USDA texture taxonomy. It reports both direct class prediction and an
indirect route that maps predicted composition through the USDA texture
triangle. The results are promising for rapid soil characterization while
remaining scoped to the controlled mixtures evaluated in the paper.
