This is the original preprint announcement from 26 February 2026. The work has
since completed peer review and been published in *IEEE Access*.

Our preprint, **“Reflectance Multispectral Imaging for Soil Composition
Estimation and USDA Texture Classification,”** is now available on
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22829). The study presents a field-deployable
multispectral imaging and machine-learning framework for soil composition
estimation and USDA texture classification. It combines 13-band reflectance
measurements with composition regression, direct USDA class prediction and
composition-based indirect classification on controlled soil mixtures.
