Our final-year research on **multispectral imaging for soil analysis** was
recognised as second runner-up at the **Manamperi Award 2025**, a national
competition for undergraduate projects in Sri Lanka.

The recognition reflects the collaborative development of a custom
multispectral acquisition system and machine-learning workflows for
agricultural sensing. No exact award day is shown because only the year has
been verified in the available project record.
