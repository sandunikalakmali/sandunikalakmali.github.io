Research on denoising diffusion probabilistic models, with two complementary
directions:

- Incorporated isotropy-inspired constraints into the diffusion training
  objective to address limitations in standard DDPM distributions and improve
  generative fidelity.
- Investigated temporal feature conditioning for diffusion-based audio
  generation to improve waveform stability, temporal coherence, and perceptual
  realism relative to DiffWave.
