Conducted multispectral-sensing research for environmental and industrial
analysis from 2023 to 2025. The work included:

- Regression models for soil-moisture estimation from spectral features.
- Classification and composition-prediction models for soil texture.
- Regression models for *Microcystis* cell-count and toxicity estimation in
  harmful algal blooms.
