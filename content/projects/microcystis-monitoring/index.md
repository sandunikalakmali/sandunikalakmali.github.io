## Research problem

Harmful algal blooms require practical monitoring methods for estimating *Microcystis* cell concentration and toxicity-related indicators.

## Contribution and methodology

This project investigates multispectral measurements and regression models for cell-count and toxicity estimation as part of broader environmental-monitoring research.
