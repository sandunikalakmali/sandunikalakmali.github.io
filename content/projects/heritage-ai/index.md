## Research problem

Historical photographs are affected by noise, limited resolution, fading, and missing colour information. A useful restoration workflow needs to address these degradations while respecting the source image.

## Contribution and methodology

Under the NVIDIA Academic Grant Program, this ongoing work develops a unified restoration pipeline for degraded Sri Lankan historical images. The current research focuses on implicit-neural-representation modules for denoising, super-resolution, and colour-guided image colorization.

## Current status

Research is in progress. No consolidated public repository or approved project figure is currently available, so the project is presented without placeholder media.
