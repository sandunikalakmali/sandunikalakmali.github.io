## Overview

HeritageAI investigates computational methods for restoring degraded Sri Lankan historical images while respecting the visual evidence that remains in each source photograph.

## Research problem and motivation

Archival photographs can combine noise, fading, low spatial resolution, missing colour information and physical damage. Treating these degradations as one generic enhancement task can obscure uncertainty or introduce unsupported detail.

## Approach and methodology

The ongoing pipeline studies implicit neural representations for denoising, super-resolution and colour-guided colorization, alongside diffusion-based restoration methods. The work is supported under the NVIDIA Academic Grant Program.

Research is in progress. No consolidated public repository or approved quantitative result is available, so this page does not claim restoration accuracy or display a generated comparison as experimental evidence.

## Technologies and methods

- Implicit neural representations
- Diffusion models
- Image denoising and super-resolution
- Colour-guided image colorization
- Computational imaging
