## Research problem

Standard denoising diffusion probabilistic models can underestimate predicted-noise energy, affecting the fidelity of generated samples.

## Contribution and methodology

The research incorporates trace-energy regularization into the diffusion training objective and evaluates its effect on generative fidelity. The linked repository contains related two-dimensional diffusion experiments rather than the complete manuscript implementation.

## Status

The associated manuscript is under review at ICLR 2027. It must not be interpreted as an accepted conference paper.
